A major thing happened in cybersecurity this October is the dramatic return of the LockBit ransomware group, which had previously been disrupted but has now launched a new variant called LockBit 5.0 (“ChuongDong”). Imagine a global game of hide-and-seek—after months of quiet, LockBit resurfaced and rapidly began attacking organizations in Europe, the Americas, and Asia.

Their new tricks aren’t limited to just one kind of computer: they hit systems running Windows, Linux, and even ESXi virtualization servers. For many security teams, it’s a chilling reminder that cybercriminals adapt quickly and keep coming back—forcing organizations to constantly rethink their defenses and recovery strategies.
